      Deal stages are CRM-specific internal codes, not proof of customer need. Revenue, buyer objections and permission to contact require their own verified sources. No demo record is sent to HubSpot.
      

      Why not build another CRM?
      GrowthFlow is a focused decision-support layer. It compares customer fit, documented buying signals, resource constraints and research uncertainty without replacing the systems a BD team already uses.
      All account research in the public demo is fictional. Live credentials must stay server-side and never be pasted into the browser or published in the project.
